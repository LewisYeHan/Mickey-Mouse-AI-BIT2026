## Part 1: Software ✅

### [1.1 Operating Systems](content/Part_1/01_Operating_Systems.md) ✅
What an OS is, kernel vs. shell, CLI vs. GUI. OS history and types, and a comparison of Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS. How an OS works: processes and process states, system calls, scheduling, deadlocks, memory, I/O and security. Practice questions with answers.

### [1.2 System Software](content/Part_1/02_System_Software.md) ✅
System vs. application software, firmware, BIOS vs. UEFI, and the boot process from power button to login screen. Kernel types, device drivers (with a printing example), utilities, and translators (assembler, compiler, interpreter). Practice questions with answers.

### [1.3 Application Software](content/Part_1/03_Application_Software.md) ✅
User space vs. kernel space, the 5-step execution lifecycle, and system calls. The 8 types of applications, and native vs. web vs. cross-platform apps. Practice questions with answers.

### [1.4 Summary and Quiz](content/Part_1/Part_1_Summary_and_Quiz.md) ✅
How Sections 1.1 to 1.3 connect, key ideas from each section, and a 13-question quiz covering recall and application, with an answer key.

### [1.5 Glossary](content/Part_1/Part_1_Glossary.md) ✅
Key terms from Sections 1.1 to 1.3, organized alphabetically, with the section where each term is explained.

## Part 2: Symbolic AI ✅

### [2.1 What Is Symbolic AI?](content/Part_2/04_What_is_Symbolic_AI.md) ✅
Introduction to Symbolic AI, its foundations, Good Old-Fashioned AI (GOFAI), symbols, explicit knowledge, and rule-based decision-making.

### [2.2 Knowledge Representation](content/Part_2/05_Knowledge_Representation.md) ✅
How AI systems represent and organize knowledge using facts, propositions, propositional logic, predicate logic, semantic networks, frames, ontologies, and knowledge graphs.

### [2.3 How Symbolic AI Reasons](content/Part_2/06_How_Symbolic_AI_Reasons.md) ✅
How AI derives conclusions from facts and rules. Covers logical inference, Modus Ponens, Modus Tollens, forward chaining, backward chaining, unification, resolution, and reasoning under uncertainty.

### [2.4 Expert Systems](content/Part_2/07_Expert_Systems.md) ✅
How expert systems work and how they are built. Covers the knowledge base, inference engine, working memory, explanation facility, knowledge acquisition, and examples such as MYCIN, DENDRAL, and XCON.

### [2.5 Strengths and Limitations](content/Part_2/08_Strengths_and_Limits.md) ✅
The advantages and limitations of Symbolic AI, including explainability, consistency, knowledge acquisition bottlenecks, ambiguity, and scalability. Compares Symbolic AI with Machine Learning and introduces Neuro-Symbolic AI.

### [2.6 Module Summary and Self-Check Quiz](content/Part_2/Part_2_Summary_and_Quiz.md) ✅
Connects the five sections of Part 2, reviews their key ideas, and provides a 13-question self-check quiz covering knowledge recall and practical application, with explanations in the answer key.

### [2.7 Glossary](content/Part_2/Part_2_Glossary.md) ✅
Key terminology from Sections 2.1 to 2.5, organized into six categories: Foundations of Symbolic AI, Knowledge Representation, Logical Reasoning, Uncertainty and Reasoning Methods, Expert Systems, and AI Approaches and Evaluation.

## 🎯 Your Mini Challenge

Think you've understood everything? Try these without looking back at the chapters.

1. **Software:** What happens between pressing the power button and seeing your login screen?
2. **Operating Systems:** Why can't an ordinary application directly control every hardware component?
3. **Symbolic AI:** How can an AI system use facts and rules to reach a conclusion?
4. **Knowledge Representation:** What's the difference between storing a fact and representing a relationship between two entities?
5. **Expert Systems:** Why does an expert system need both a knowledge base and an inference engine?
