Where each topic of the final Markdown content appears in our other learning resources. The Markdown is the source; the other resources were built from it.

* **PPT:** PPT Slides V1.0, 159 slides (submitted under *PPT Slides V1.0*)

**GAP** means the topic is not covered in that resource. *Not selected* means it was left out on purpose.

Lab numbers in the SG column are the Study Guide's own (for example SG Lab 1.4). Labs in the final Markdown are numbered by their section, so Lab 1.1 is the lab at the end of Section 1.1.
